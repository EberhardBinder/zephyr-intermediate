
Latency: measures the time intervall between timer expiry and processing of this timer event.

Definition of the guarantee: if the latency is greater than the deadline (10ms), a warning ist written in the log.

Root cause: in the original version, thread maintenance has a higher priority (4) than thread control(7).
After timer expiry, both threads are runnable. The thread with the higher priority, maintenance, is scheduled first.
Only after thread maintenance has finished, thread control is scheduled. Thread control is therefore delayed and misses its deadline.

Priority change fixes it: now thread maintenance has a lower priority (9) than thread control(7).
After timer expiry, both threads are runnable. The thread with the higher priority, control, is scheduled first and hits its deadline.
After thread control has finished, thread maintenance is scheduled.
